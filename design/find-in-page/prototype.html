Harbor Authority winter tide notes

The harbor authority publishes revised tide tables twice a year. The winter edition covers the inner harbor, the fuel dock and the outer breakwater, and it replaces every earlier printing.

Pilots should note that the harbor entrance now closes to vessels over forty meters when the swell passes two meters. A harbor master is on call from five in the morning until the last ferry docks.

Mooring fees for the winter season are unchanged. Berths on the north quay are assigned by the harbor office, and a berth left empty for ten days returns to the waiting list.

Fuel is sold at the harbor fuel dock between eight and four. Credit is not extended, and the office cannot hold deliveries for crews that have not signed in.

The ferry timetable follows the tides, not the clock. When the low water falls after noon, the afternoon crossing leaves from the outer harbor instead, and the harbor shuttle runs every twenty minutes.

Visitors can read the full notice at the harbor office or ask at the quay. The library keeps older tables, and the museum shows how the channel was dredged. Questions go to the harbor desk.

Closing note: the harbor lights are tested every Sunday. Report any dark lamp along the breakwater to the duty officer at once.
