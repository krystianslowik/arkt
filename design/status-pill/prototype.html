Notes from the quay

The winter tide tables are out, and the pilots' association has a short summary for crews who only need the dates.

If the entrance closes for swell, the shuttle timetable changes the same morning. The harbor office keeps a dredging report for anyone curious about the channel.

Berth holders can renew from the renewal page until the end of the month. Visitors should read the rules of the quay before they arrive, and call ahead when the swell is forecast above two meters.

Fuel prices for the week are posted on the dock. Questions about tolls, lights or the ferry go to the desk; the duty officer answers every message before noon.
