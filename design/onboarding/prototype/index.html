Tip 1 of 5

Open a tab.

Press Command-T to search or type an address. Command-L edits the address of the current tab.

Tip 2 of 5

Close by archiving.

Command-W closes a tab and keeps it in the Archive, so you can find it again.

Tip 3 of 5

Switch Spaces.

Swipe left or right with two fingers on the sidebar to move between Spaces.

Tip 4 of 5

Split the view.

Drag a tab from the sidebar onto the page to see two tabs side by side.

Tip 5 of 5

Keep Favorites close.

Drag a tab to the top of the sidebar to make it a Favorite.
