Three days in Lisbon

Seven hills, one yellow tram and a pastry habit you will not be able to shake. A walkable plan for a long weekend.

Inês Duarte, 8 minute read

Start high. The castle walls catch the first light before the river does, and by nine the lanes of Alfama below are already loud with deliveries and radios through open windows.

Day one: Alfama and the river

Walk down rather than up. Every staircase ends somewhere worth stopping, and the best lunch is usually the one with no menu outside.
